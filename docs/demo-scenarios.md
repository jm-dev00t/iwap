# IWAP Demo Scenarios

## 1. Monthly Sales Report

Command: "이번 달 매출 보고서 만들어서 슬랙 채널과 이메일로 보내줘"

Customer value: Reduces repetitive month-end reporting from hours to minutes. The demo shows report generation, Slack delivery, Email delivery, validation, and audit logging in one clear flow.

## 2. New Customer Onboarding

Command: "신규 고객 등록 후 환영 이메일 보내고, CRM에 기록하고, 담당자에게 알림"

Customer value: Prevents missed handoffs after sales conversion. The demo shows CRM logging, welcome email, owner notification, and a traceable onboarding workflow.

## 3. Low Inventory Purchasing Alert

Command: "재고 부족 제품 리스트 뽑아서 구매팀 카카오톡으로 보내"

Customer value: Helps operations teams catch purchasing needs before stockouts. The demo shows inventory analysis, approval-ready purchasing context, and KakaoWork-style notification.

## 4. Weekly Sales Performance PDF

Command: "주간 영업 실적 분석해서 PDF 리포트 생성 후 공유"

Customer value: Gives managers a repeatable weekly performance ritual. The demo shows sales ranking, conversion summary, report artifact creation, and sharing.
